# Analysis Brief: Nova Markets (NOVA-FX) Performance

## What's going on
Nova Markets is partially correct: their merchant portal approval rate dropped from 79.62% (Apr–May) to 72.39% in June. However, core card and e-wallet performance in established markets remained stable. The overall drop was driven by two distinct operational factors:

1. New Market Expansion (NBLX-07): On June 2, route NBLX-07 went live for IDR and VND bank transfers. Following risk rule tightening on June 8, this route yielded a lower processor approval rate (~40.6%). Because this new stream represented 28% of June's total attempts, it heavily diluted the baseline average.
2. Japanese Card BIN Changes (SORVA-14)**: On June 11, acquirer BIN reallocations caused JPY card approval rates on SORVA-14 to drop from 70.16% to 61.16%.
3. Portal Dashboard Metric Design**: The merchant portal excludes risk-filtered attempts. On NBLX-07, heavy pre-routing risk filtering artificially distorted the visible attempt-to-approval ratio.

## What we should do
* Merchant Messaging: Inform Nova Markets that the rate drop reflects expansion into new regions (IDR/VND) rather than degradation of existing routes. Highlight that NBLX-07 added over $355,000 USD in incremental approved deposits.
* Routing Optimization: Reroute JPY card traffic from SORVA-14 to KRTX-03 (which maintains an 82.11% approval rate).
* Internal Changes: Update the merchant portal documentation or metrics to clarify how risk-filtered transactions are handled.

## Assumptions and open questions
* Assumptions: Extracted complete days (June 1–28) for trend accuracy, omitting partial extract dates (June 29–30).
* Open Questions: Can NBLX-07 risk threshold rules be tuned per sub-merchant segment to reduce false positives without increasing chargeback risk?
